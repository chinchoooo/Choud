# Code of conduct

Choud is a small project that people run at home, often for their own family. We want contributing to
it to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our standard

Behaviour that helps the community:

- being kind and patient, especially with people new to self-hosting, Linux or programming,
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully,
- assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear,
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them,
- focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory jokes or language,
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention,
- trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly pinging people who have said no,
- publishing someone else's private information (including their server address or files) without
  permission,
- anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review) and when someone
is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove
comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, mute or
ban a person for a time or permanently.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting (the repository's **Security > Report a
vulnerability** form works for conduct reports too, and only maintainers see it) or contact a maintainer
directly on GitHub. Reports are handled privately and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith can be removed from the project by the
other maintainers.
